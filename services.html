1. Newborn & Neonatal Care (0 – 28 Days)

Comprehensive transition care for delicate newborns, preemies, and infants.

NICU Warmers Equipped

The first month of a baby's life requires watchful medical attention. At Shreeji Hospital, Dr. Devang Gokalani performs detailed newborn screening, umbilical stump evaluation, head circumference and weight tracking, and timely intervention for neonatal jaundice.

Equipped with infant radiant warmers and LED phototherapy units, we manage physiological jaundice right here in New Ranip without separating the infant from the mother.

Common Newborn Conditions Treated:

  • ✓ Neonatal Hyperbilirubinemia (Jaundice): Phototherapy monitoring and safe bilirubin clearance.
  • ✓ Low Birth Weight / Preterm Feeding: Kangaroo mother care guidance & customized latch assistance.
  • ✓ Colic & Reflux: Gentle infant gas management without harmful sedatives.
  • ✓ Umbilical Granuloma & Sepsis Prevention: Sterile neonatal umbilical chord hygiene.

2. Painless Childhood Immunizations & Cold-Chain Vaccines

Following Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) & WHO cold-chain protocols.

100% Cold-Chain Stored

Vaccines are the single most effective shield protecting your child from dangerous infectious diseases. We offer both painless (acellular DTaP) and traditional combinations to protect against Polio, Hepatitis A & B, Rotavirus, Pneumococcal pneumonia, MMR, Typhoid, and Chickenpox.

All vaccines at Shreeji Hospital are monitored under strict 2°C to 8°C temperature logs with emergency generator backup to guarantee maximum antibody response.

Why Parents Choose Our Vaccine Clinic:

  • ✓ Painless DTaP Options: Low fever incidence and significantly reduced swelling.
  • ✓ Digital Immunization Record: Parents receive a clear card with upcoming milestone dates.
  • ✓ Post-Vaccination Guidance: Exact dosage instructions for mild reactions or irritability.
  • ✓ Catch-Up Immunization: Specialized schedule for missed or delayed shots.

3. Pediatric Respiratory Care & Nebulization

Rapid relief for childhood wheezing, persistent night cough, and asthma flare-ups.

On-Site Nebulization

Ahmedabad’s urban air quality and seasonal temperature shifts often trigger acute reactive airway disease, bronchiolitis, and dust-mite allergies in young children.

Dr. Devang Gokalani provides clinical auscultation, instant nebulization therapy, pulse-oximetry saturation monitoring, and customized long-term asthma action plans with inhaler/spacer technique coaching for parents.

Respiratory Symptoms Evaluated:

  • ✓ Wheezing & Chest Retraction: Rapid bronchodilator therapy to restore comfortable airflow.
  • ✓ Persistent Nocturnal Cough: Ruling out allergic bronchitis or GERD reflux.
  • ✓ Allergic Rhinitis: Clear nasal congestion, sneezing, and watery eye management.
  • ✓ SpO2 Oxygen Monitoring: Continuous verification of healthy blood oxygenation.

4. Childhood Fevers, Viral Infections & Dengue Care

Prompt diagnosis, fever management, and prevention of dehydration.

Emergency Fever Care

A sudden high spike in fever causes immense anxiety for parents. Dr. Devang Gokalani is widely appreciated in New Ranip for taking time to calm anxious parents, conducting focused laboratory investigations (CBC, Dengue NS1, Malaria, Typhoid widal), and avoiding harmful indiscriminate antibiotic misuse.

For children unable to keep oral fluids down, our hospital provides immediate intravenous hydration and supportive pediatric ward care.

Common Pediatric Infections Managed:

  • ✓ Viral Fevers & Influenza: Safe paracetamol dosing, cooling sponge guidance, and rest.
  • ✓ Dengue & Platelet Monitoring: Close watch on hematocrit and platelet counts.
  • ✓ Acute Gastroenteritis (Diarrhea/Vomiting): WHO-ORS protocol & zinc supplementation.
  • ✓ Hand, Foot & Mouth Disease (HFMD): Soothing oral ulcers and skin lesions.

5. Child Growth Percentiles & Milestone Tracking

Monitoring physical, motor, language, and cognitive growth milestones.

Developmental Review

Every child develops at their own unique pace, but early identification of developmental delays can make a life-changing difference.

Dr. Devang tracks height-for-age, weight-for-age, and head circumference percentiles against WHO growth standards, while advising mothers on balanced weaning foods, iron-rich nutrition, and speech development.

Key Milestone Checks:

  • ✓ Motor Milestones: Neck holding (3 mo), sitting (6-8 mo), standing & walking (12 mo).
  • ✓ Language & Social: Social smile (2 mo), babbling (6 mo), first words & eye contact (12 mo).
  • ✓ Nutritional Deficiencies: Early detection of Vitamin D rickets or anemia.
  • ✓ Picky Eater Solutions: Practical recipes to satisfy nutritional needs.
